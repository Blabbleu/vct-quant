# Frozen batch BT as fourth stack input

## Pre-registration (written before scoring)

Hypothesis: the preregistered batch Bradley–Terry (BT) probability adds complementary calibration to the frozen three-input Tier-1 stack. The four inputs, in order, are production Elo, frozen round-share, frozen lineup skill, and frozen batch BT. Join by unique match ID, require exact one-to-one coverage, equal binary labels and team-A/team-B orientation, finite pre-match probabilities, and equality of the independently replayed production Elo probabilities. Existing round-share and lineup fallbacks remain equal to Elo. The BT replay uses its frozen maps cell: half-life 365 days and strength penalty 1.0; teams without history start at strength zero.

Fit one no-intercept logistic stack on completed binary Tier-1 matches from **2023–2024 only**. Minimize mean binary log loss plus `0.001 * ||w - [1,0,0,0]||² / 2`, using the existing stack fit helper. Freeze the four weights. Score 2025 and 2026-to-date separately on identical Tier-1 binary rows for production Elo, the original three-input stack with its 2023–24 frozen fit, and the new four-input stack. No tuning, refitting, or selection on either test year; do not use the live prediction log.

Primary metric: per-match binary log loss, with paired t computed from Elo loss minus candidate loss. Report n, mean log loss, Brier score, accuracy, ten-bin calibration and expected calibration error (ECE) for all three forecasts in each test year. **Verdict for the four-input stack:** WIN only if its paired t against Elo is greater than +2 in both years and ECE is no worse than Elo in both years. If both t values are positive but the WIN condition fails, NOT PROVEN. Otherwise REJECTED.

The batch BT optimizer is frozen at L-BFGS-B `maxiter=20` and accepts finite strengths without checking its success flag; some fits may stop before convergence. Changing that limit would define a different experiment, so this test leaves the solver and frozen cell unchanged and records the caveat. Both 2025 and 2026 have been consulted in prior research. This retrospective comparison is not a clean prospective holdout.

### Verdict

**NOT PROVEN.** The paired t values are positive in both 2025 (+1.631) and 2026 (+1.220), but neither exceeds +2; 2026 ECE (0.067757) also exceeds Elo ECE (0.064583). The BT solver retains its frozen `maxiter=20` and finite-output-only check, so optimization convergence is not guaranteed. These previously consulted years provide retrospective evidence only, not a clean prospective holdout. Production Elo remains unchanged.
