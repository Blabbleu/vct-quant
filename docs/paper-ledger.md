# Paper ledger: frozen entry rule (dev API)

`GET /api/paper-ledger` is a read-only, hypothetical accounting diagnostic; it is **not** a betting recommendation, order, fill, executable return, or live odds feed. It uses the append-only `prediction_log.parquet` and the canonical DB result rows. It does not alter `vct update` or any forecast.

Rule fixed before evaluating outcomes: one flat **1-unit** entry per numeric-ID Tier-1 match at the **first** logged pre-start snapshot where the absolute primary Elo–market midpoint gap reaches **10 percentage points**, spread is **≤10 points**, market volume is **≥1,000**, and both a contract slug and valid probabilities exist. The chosen side is whichever the primary Elo favors relative to the midpoint. Missing liquidity/identity or contradictory simultaneous first quotes skip the entry; a later qualified snapshot may enter if earlier quotes did not qualify. Entries never revise their side or price after seeing later quotes/results. Volume is an exchange market aggregate, **not proof of depth at the quoted price**.

For an illustrative conservative price, estimate the chosen side's ask as `midpoint + spread/2`, with the opposite-side midpoint `1 - p_market_a`. A hypothetical win returns `1/ask - 1` net units, a loss `-1`; no fees, slippage, execution, partial fills, market depth, or dispute/void semantics are modeled. Profit is not a realizable trading result. Canonical settlement requires both exact numeric team IDs, dated Tier-1 result, complete plausible series map scores, and mutually consistent winner flags. Missing results stay `open`; ambiguous existing results are `unverified` with null P&L. Historical completion times often mean calendar days, so a result dated before the entry **day** cannot settle; same-day results still require external verification if a fixture was rescheduled.

Sampled price movement uses only a later pre-start quote with the same match ID, team orientation and market contract, passing the same liquidity filters and occurring before **both** its own and the frozen entry's scheduled kickoff. `sampled_clv` is last comparable midpoint minus entry midpoint for the selected side. It is **not true closing-line value**: these ~2-hourly refreshes may miss closing prices. A missing comparable later quote gives null, not zero. A spread-adjusted entry price and sampled midpoint are different quantities; never call their difference executable CLV.
