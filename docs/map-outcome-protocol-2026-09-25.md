# Conditional played-map outcome diagnostic — frozen protocol

Scope is **map outcomes only**, conditional on the map name of each *played* map. Historical map names lack a pre-series timestamp; these rows must never be used to claim a pre-veto series-forecast improvement. A Bo3 ending 2–0 omits its decider. Neither the series result nor within-series map 1 outcome may inform map 2's pre-series prediction.

- Iterate canonical `match_sequence()` in match-ID order, Tier-1 Elo K=48 and Tier-2 K=0, map-share update as in production. Attach each match's **pre-match** Elo series probability; invert the independent Bo3/Bo5 series formula for a common-map baseline. Keep only Tier-1 Bo3/Bo5 with valid, non-drawn scored played-map rows and named maps; do not infer any missing map. Use official year from completion date, not Kaggle year folders.
- Candidate is the already frozen, optional known-veto map Elo (`features/maps.py`): map-specific binary Elo K=8 and weight `min(team map counts)/(min(counts)+160)` blended in Elo-difference space with global map strength. Update every map-specific rating **after recording all predictions for the entire series**. No sweep, no tuning on this diagnostic; global Elo still updates after each match only.
- Score both probabilities on the *identical maps* by year (2023–24 validation, 2025 and 2026 retrospective). Show log loss, Brier, number of maps and unique series, and paired map-loss t. Also report series-clustered paired t because maps within a series are correlated. A gain on the retrospectively consulted years is exploratory, not clean holdout. No primary forecast change follows from this experiment.
- Selection/decision, fixed before execution: if 2023–24 validation does not improve, stop, report rejection. If it improves, score 2025–26 without changing any settings. Only a positive validation and positive retrospective result with series-clustered |t| > 2 merits **prospective collection of timestamped full veto data**, not promotion. Otherwise reject or label not proven. Primary Elo and its pre-registered shadow promotion rule stay untouched.
